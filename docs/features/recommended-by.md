# Recommended by (§16.9)

Per item: who or what recommended it (free text with autocomplete), optional note and date — inside
“More details” in Simple Mode. More → **Loans & people** shows counts per person (“Sam: 7 in pile,
3 completed”) and the items they recommended; rename, merge, and **Forget this person** (shared with loans).
Names stay out of share pages, share cards and logs; included in exports and backups.
