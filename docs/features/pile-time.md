# Pile Time

How long your unfinished pile (Planned, In Progress, On Hold) would take, with an honest source and confidence for every number.

**Where:** the Pile page (total, per type, and per confidence), the item page (**Time** panel), **What fits?** on the Pile page, and **Finish within** in Pick for Me.

## Estimate sources, best first

| Source | Confidence | Remaining time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your own estimate (item page → Add my estimate) | High | Estimate minus hours played or episodes watched |
| IGDB typical time ("normally"; on request, games only) | Medium | Same rule |
| Movie runtime | High | Full runtime until completed |
| Episodes × episode length | Medium, or Low when the length is assumed (45 min TV, 24 min anime) | Unwatched episodes only |
| Audiobook length | High | Minus progress % |
| Pages × 1.5 min/page (assumed pace) | Low | Minus pages read |

Items with no estimate are **counted separately** as "no estimate yet", never as zero. HowLongToBeat isn't used: it has no API, and scraping isn't allowed.
