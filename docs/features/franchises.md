# Series, franchises and "Next in series"

- **Relations:** link two items as sequel, prequel, spin-off, side story, adaptation, remake or same franchise. Relations are stored once and shown from both sides (a sequel on one item appears as a prequel on the other).
- **Suggestions:** anime sources report related titles, which appear under *Series & related → suggested by the data source*. **Link** connects them when the title is in your library; **Dismiss** hides them. Nothing is linked automatically, and a dismissed suggestion never comes back on refresh.
- **Series (franchises):** an ordered list of items with a "main entry" flag. Side entries are never "next".
- **Next in series:** with "in order" on, it's the in-progress main entry, or else the first unfinished main entry after the furthest completed one. With it off, it's the first unfinished main entry.
- **Pick for Me → Only the next entry of ordered series** skips later entries. Recommendations give a bonus to the next entry.
