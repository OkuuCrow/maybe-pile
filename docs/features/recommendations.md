# Recommendations ("From your pile")

These are computed **locally** from your own library. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no outside data is used.

Signals come from items you finished and rated 4★ or higher. Each signal earns points and shows its reason:

| Signal | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Next in a series you follow | +4 |
| Same creator or studio | +3 |
| Same series | +3 |
| Shared genres (up to 2) | +1 each |
| A mood you often finish | +1 |
| Can be finished in about 2 hours | +1 |
| On your pile for over a year | +1 |

- Only **Planned** and **On Hold** items are recommended, and each needs at least one taste signal: "short" or "old" alone isn't enough.
- **Cold start:** with no 4★+ completions, the card says "Rate a few finished items 4★ or higher to get suggestions."
- **Not now** hides an item for 30 days; **Not interested** hides it until you reset (Settings → Features → Recommendations).
