# Ownership, subscriptions and Leaving Soon

- **Item page → Ownership & access:** record whether you own it (digital or physical), have it on a subscription, a library loan, or borrowed it, or have it on a wishlist. Each entry can name a service and an **available until** date.
- **Services** (Settings → Features → Services and stores): marking a subscription inactive when you cancel it removes its items from "accessible now".
- **Leaving Soon** (Pile page): unfinished items whose access ends within 14 days by default (configurable; an entry can set its own reminder window).
- **Notifications** are opt-in. They fire only while the app is open, at most once per entry and end date. Nothing is fetched from streaming catalogs; everything here is entered by you.
